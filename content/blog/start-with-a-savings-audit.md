Buying AI software is easy to justify and hard to unwind. A license feels like progress. It is not progress if the work you hoped to remove is still with the same people, plus a new login.

## What the audit is for

The [free AI savings audit](/contact) is a conversation, not a pitch. You describe the company and the work that costs the most: the tasks that eat time or create rework. That is enough to separate three kinds of work.

1. Work that is repetitive enough to hand to an agent, with a person still approving the outcome.
2. Work that needs a written roadmap before anyone builds, because it crosses teams or systems.
3. Work that should stay as it is, because the ambiguity is the actual job.

The third category matters. A useful audit is allowed to end with "do not spend money on this yet."

## What happens after

If there is a real target, the next paid step is sized to it.

- An assessment and roadmap is the right move when several functions are involved and the sequence is not obvious.
- An implementation project is the right move when the workflow is already clear.
- Training is the right move when the team will live with the tools.
- A retainer is the right move when something is in production and needs continued implementation.

Published starting points for those engagements are on the [services page](/services). They are ranges because the work varies. They are not a quote for your company until the workflow has been seen.

There is a second way to pay for an implementation. On a workflow whose cost can be counted, the fee can be 25% of the measured savings, and you keep the other 75%. The audit names that cost, the measurement, and whether a fixed price or the share is the better fit.

## What to bring

You do not need a data platform or an AI strategy document. You need a clear account of where time goes. The audit form asks for industry, company size, and the work that costs the most time or money on purpose. Those answers do more for a first conversation than a list of models.

[Book a free AI audit](/contact) when you want that conversation. Keep secrets, customer lists, and passwords out of the form. The first pass only needs enough detail to know whether a conversation is worth having.
