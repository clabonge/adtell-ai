We help a business implement AI automation against a named task. A person at the business still reviews what the agent prepares.

## How the automation works

The agent takes the repeatable first pass. It drafts, looks up an answer the business has already written, moves information between tools, or prepares material a person still reviews. That person remains responsible for the customer, for anything that spends or collects money, and for anything that would be embarrassing or unsafe if it were wrong.

Website creation and management is part of each implementation.

## How a workflow gets chosen

We start from a named task, not from a model. One workflow is scoped until a careful person could follow the procedure. The scope includes what the agent may prepare. If the approval step cannot be described, the workflow does not ship.

## What this page reports

This page reports goals and the shape of the work. Hours, dollars, and before-and-after comparisons would have to be measured and agreed before we would publish them.
