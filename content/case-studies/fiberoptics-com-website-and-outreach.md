We helped FiberOptics.com implement AI automation with the goal of updating website content, social media, customer outreach, and product recommendations. FiberOptics.com is the product and shop division of Adtell Inc. The public shop is [fiberoptics.com](https://fiberoptics.com).

## Before

Website content, social media, customer outreach, and product recommendations were updated by a person.

## The workflow

Adtell.ai took over those updates. The workflows use Outlook, Shopify, Teams, SharePoint, and QuickBooks. Shopify is the store.

## A person stays in charge

A person reviews and approves what goes out. The automation does not send it on its own. If the approval step cannot be described, the workflow does not ship.

## What changed

The measured productivity gain is 2.5 times previous efficiency across the combined work of FiberOptics.com and The Fiber School. That gain also relates to direct cost savings.
