We helped The Fiber School implement AI automation with the goal of student communications about expiring certifications, upselling additional classes, outreach, and social media. The Fiber School is the training division of Adtell Inc. The public site is [thefiberschool.com](https://thefiberschool.com).

## Before

Student communications about expiring certifications, upselling additional classes, outreach, and social media were prepared by a person.

## The workflow

Adtell.ai took over those updates. The workflows use Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, and QuickBooks.

## A person stays in charge

A person reviews and approves what goes out. The automation does not send it on its own. If the approval step cannot be described, the workflow does not ship.

## What changed

The measured productivity gain is 2.5 times previous efficiency. That gain also relates to direct cost savings.
