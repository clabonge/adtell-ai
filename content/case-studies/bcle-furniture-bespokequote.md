We helped BCLE Furniture implement AI automation with the goal of automating quoting and inventory management. Adtell.ai delivered [BespokeQuote.furniture](https://bespokequote.furniture) for that work.

## What we delivered

BespokeQuote.furniture is the quoting and inventory system delivered at BCLE Furniture. Clients upload drawings: DWG, DXF, PDF, and sketches. A person reviews a quote priced from real materials and labor. The job is approved with a PO and deposit. Status and messages live in a dashboard.

## A person stays in charge

A person reviews the quote. The job is tracked in the dashboard after that review.

## What changed

Measured result. Before, a quote could take up to three days for review and quoting. Now the maximum turnaround is 24 hours.
